🌌 About the Game

NEXUS: The Infinite Realm is an ambitious 2D action RPG inspired by anime storytelling, challenging Soulslike combat, mysterious worlds, powerful bosses, and cinematic experiences.

Explore a semi-open world, master combat abilities, discover hidden secrets, and face formidable enemies in a realm shaped by ancient gods.

The project aims to deliver a balanced Soulslike experience with side-scrolling gameplay and mobile-friendly touch controls.

⚔️ Planned Gameplay Features

- Side-scrolling exploration.
- Stamina-based combat.
- Dodge and defensive mechanics.
- Melee attacks and special abilities.
- Challenging enemy encounters.
- Powerful boss battles.
- Checkpoints and progression.
- Character experience and upgrades.
- Cinematic story sequences.
- Mobile-friendly control system.
- Offline gameplay.

Features are planned and will be marked complete as they are implemented and tested.

🌍 The Infinite Realm

The story takes place in a mysterious universe connected to ancient powers and the Age of Gods.

Key characters and lore:

- Aruvex — A central character in the NEXUS universe.
- Voranth — A character connected to the realm's mythology.
- Kálika — A significant figure in the ancient lore.
- The Age of Gods — The foundational mythology of the universe.

More story details, locations, characters, and enemies will be revealed as development progresses.
